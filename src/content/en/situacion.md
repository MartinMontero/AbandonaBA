This is general information. The Spanish page has the full detail and every source.

**Confirmed by several outlets.** Around Nov 17–18, 2026, a video circulated of Ricardo Salinas Pliego, majority shareholder of Grupo Elektra, at a dinner. He called the minimum-wage rise and the 40-hour week an "estupidez increíble" and an "ataque al empleo", and said machines could work without unions or leave.

**Not confirmed.** Banco Azteca and Grupo Salinas made no official announcement, headcount, timetable or budget as of Nov 22, 2026. The figure of 15,000 tellers is his own. His claim that he has already started reducing tellers is his own statement as reported.

**Later softening, as reported.** A later statement attributed to him says technology does not take work from people who prepare. We could not find the original, so it is unverified.

If your job disappears, Article 439 of the Federal Labor Law requires an agreement or tribunal authorization and sets a minimum payment. We do not say the bank will dismiss anyone. See the [Spanish page](/situacion/).
